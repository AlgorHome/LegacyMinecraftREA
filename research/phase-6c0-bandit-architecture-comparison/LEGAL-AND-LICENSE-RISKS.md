## Research boundary

UPSTREAM_DOCUMENTED: the custom terms permit private research/internal work but restrict public copying, mirrors, derivative distribution and packaged releases without written permission; third-party components retain their own licenses. This phase applies the owner's stricter boundary: no source copy/fork/archive/patch import, build or execution. Only factual paraphrases, URLs and text-content hashes retained. No Bandit binaries, runtime images, custom assets or credentials obtained.

General API concepts, content-addressed caching, adapter boundaries and per-stage evidence can be specified independently from official APIs. That does not grant rights in Bandit's particular source/patches/assets. Do not translate its implementation line by line, remove notices or relabel its custom shim as upstream GLFW. Independently licensed upstream dependencies should be obtained from their own source/distribution with exact version/notice checks under a separate approval.

Source-comparison hashes show six sampled production files already match upstream. This review neither introduces that overlap nor resolves its origin or permission. Before public LegacyForge redistribution, establish pre-existing grants, authorship/derivation and third-party notices. No accusation of infringement or assumption that a new permission is definitely required is justified without those records. Research exposure also means this report cannot certify an unexposed clean-room process; future design must document independent specifications and code provenance.

## Publication and authentication

Do not republish Bandit APPX/runtime images or source patches. Conflicting prerelease-guide wording across docs is unresolved; this internal architecture research does not distribute a guide for deploying Bandit. Credit and visible official links are retained for factual mentions without implying endorsement.

All future Minecraft use must preserve legitimate Microsoft/Xbox authentication and Minecraft ownership/token checks. Java-only diagnostics do not launch Minecraft and need no account, which is not an alternate game-authentication path. No bypass instructions or code were extracted or proposed. Production auth remains unchanged.
